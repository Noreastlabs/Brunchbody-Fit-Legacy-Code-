# Beta Program Operations - 2026-06-13

## Owner Gate

The beta program remains gated until the owner records explicit approval for scope, tester instructions, privacy wording, and release artifacts. The signed public app release remains blocked until RC2 signed Android/iOS artifacts and explicit owner approvals are recorded.

## Tester Scope

Testers should use Brunch Body as a local-first personal organization and tracking app. The beta should focus on onboarding, Settings, export clarity, Delete local data clarity, journal/nutrition/recreation flows, and confusing wording.

No tester should be asked to send exported files, screenshots with private details, sensitive health information, or device-local app data. Tester feedback can describe what happened, what screen was involved, expected behavior, actual behavior, and whether the issue repeated.

## Intake Path

Use existing Support & Contact or release/tester materials for feedback. Do not introduce a new support chat, account recovery channel, cloud recovery promise, remote deletion path, or analytics workflow.

## Beta Checklist

- Confirm local-only guardrails pass.
- Confirm secret and signing-material guardrails pass.
- Confirm Help & Support, Privacy & Data, export, and Delete local data copy match current behavior.
- Confirm known unsupported behavior is named plainly: no Brunch Body cloud sync, no automatic Brunch Body backup or restore, no AI runtime, and no professional health guidance.
- Record owner approval before expanding tester scope.

## Non-Claims

This artifact does not approve public launch, monetization, broad platform expansion, backend sync, medical guidance, AI runtime behavior, or store submission.
